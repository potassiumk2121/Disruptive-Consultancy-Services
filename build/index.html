WHO WE ARE

Disruptive Consultancy Services Pvt. Ltd (DCSPL) was established in 2022 in Delhi, India by a highly qualified and experienced economist, project manager, financial analyst and social scientist, Mr. Umesh Narwadeshwar Tiwari.

Mr. Umesh Tiwari has over 30 years of experience leading multi-million dollar rural and urban development monitoring and verification programs in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Yemen and supported by large international and national teams for the World Bank, KfW, US Agency for International Development (USAID), European Commission, World Health Organization (WHO), UNICEF, Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation, Michael and Susan Dell Foundation, and other donors across a wide range of activities and sectors, including (i) infrastructure and non- infrastructure project monitoring and asset verification, (ii) market assessment and value chain analysis, (iii) agricultural value chain analysis, (iv) watershed management, (v) economic impact analysis of water resource projects, (vi) survey design and data collection, (vii) monitoring and evaluation, (viii) cost benefit analysis for infrastructure projects, (ix) solid waste management (SWM), (x) environmental and social safeguard (ESS) management, (xi) stakeholder consultation, (xii) grant management, (xiii) logistics and operation management, (xiv) administration of disposition funds, (xv) financial management systems, (xvi) capacity building, (xvii) community-based intervention, (xviii) institutional development, (xix) Disaster Risk management, (xx) greivance redressal mechanisms and, (xxi) development of software for asset mapping and verification.

DCSPL has the technical and operation capability to implement medium and large size programs in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, India, Nepal, and Yemen with its' qualified and experience team based in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, India, Nepal, and Yemen.
